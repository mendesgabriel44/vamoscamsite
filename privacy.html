VAMOS!cam — Privacy Policy (iPhone)

Last updated: 26 October 2026

VAMOS!cam is an instant-replay camera for racquet and court sports. While the app is open it keeps the camera recording into a short temporary buffer in the iPhone's memory and, when you ask (the on-screen button, a Bluetooth remote, the volume buttons or a raised-arm gesture), it saves the last seconds as a video in the app's Library.

In short: VAMOS!cam does not collect, sell or share any personal data. Your videos stay on your iPhone unless you share them.

What the app uses, and why

What Why Where it stays
Camera To keep the replay buffer and save the clips you ask for The buffer lives in memory and is discarded when you leave the app; saved clips stay in the app on your iPhone
Microphone (optional) To record the sound of the clip. Without it the app records video only Same as the video
Photos (add only) Only when you choose Move to Photos, Export a clip from the editor or Save a vertical video. The app cannot read your photo library In your Photos
Local network (optional, off by default) The Highlights page: people on the same Wi-Fi or on your iPhone's Personal Hotspot can watch and download today's clips in their browser Your local network only; nothing goes to the internet
Settings Clip length, video profile, gesture, remote key, button positions and similar On your iPhone

Processing that happens on your iPhone only

  • Arm gesture (optional, off by default): the app looks at the live camera image a few times per second to recognise a raised arm and save the clip. No image is stored or sent anywhere.
  • Vertical video: when you create one, the app analyses the clip on the device to find the ball and the players and builds a 9:16 copy. Nothing is uploaded.
  • Compression (on by default): after a clip is saved, while the phone is cool, the app re-encodes it into a smaller HEVC file on the device and replaces the original. You can turn it off in Settings → Storage.
  • Player zoom on the Library card: when a clip is saved by the gesture, a small still of the person who raised the arm is kept on the device to show on the Library card.

What the app does NOT do

  • No account, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no third-party SDKs.
  • It does not send videos, audio, images, location or any device identifier to the developer or to anyone else.
  • It does not read your location, contacts or existing photos.

Highlights on the local network

If you turn it on, the app opens a page on your local network and shows a QR code and a link. Anyone with the link who is on the same network (the same Wi-Fi, or your iPhone's Personal Hotspot) can watch and download today's clips. The link contains a random key created when you turn the feature on and erased when you turn it off. The connection is plain HTTP on your local network, so use it only on a network you trust. If you type your Personal Hotspot's name and password to show a "join this Wi-Fi" QR code, they are stored only on your iPhone (the password in the Keychain) and never leave it. Turn the feature off at any time and the link stops working immediately.

Sharing

A video leaves your iPhone only when you share it (the Share button, AirDrop, WhatsApp, Instagram and so on) or move it to Photos. The error report (Settings → Diagnostics) is plain text (device model, iOS and app version, settings and log lines; no videos or photos) and is sent only if you tap Share and pick a destination. The rules of the app you choose then apply.

Deleting your data

Delete a clip in the VAMOS!cam Library. Deleting the app removes all clips that are still in the app, its settings and everything it stored. Clips you moved or exported to Photos stay in Photos.

Children

The app is not directed at children and does not collect data from anyone.
